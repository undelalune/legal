## 1. What the App is

The App is a reference for looking up codes and texts of the International Classification of Diseases, Eleventh Revision (ICD-11), Mortality and Morbidity Statistics (MMS), offline and in several languages.

> **The App does not diagnose anything and is not medical advice.** It shows the classification as published. Choosing a code is your professional decision, and the App is not a substitute for clinical judgement or for the coding rules that apply where you work.

ICD-11 is not yet the classification for official reporting in every country. Where ICD-10 or a national modification is still required (in Poland, for example), use the classification your authority prescribes.
